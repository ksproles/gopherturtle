Privacy Policy

Effective October 4, 2026

TwoCents is an app for sharing short voice memos with close friends, the people who follow you, or everyone. This policy explains what information TwoCents collects, how it’s used, who can see it, and the choices you have. If you have questions, email owner@snacktimemedia.net.

What we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: your email address, a password, your name and your @handle, plus an optional profile photo, bio and link you choose to add. Your profile photo, bio and link can be seen by anyone signed in to TwoCents. Passwords are handled by our sign-in provider (Google Firebase Authentication); we never see or store them ourselves.
  • What you post: voice recordings, captions, comments, likes and reposts.
  • Your connections: who you follow, who follows you, follow requests you send or receive, your close friends list and your blocked list.
  • Reports: if you report a memo, comment or person, we keep the report, the reason you chose and a copy of the reported caption or comment so we can review it.

Information kept only on your device

  • Your playback speed setting and a short history of memos you’ve listened to, used to tailor Discover. This stays in your browser or phone and isn’t sent to us.

TwoCents only uses your microphone while you’re recording a memo, and only opens your camera or photos when you pick a profile photo (we upload just the one photo you choose). We don’t collect your location or contacts, and we don’t use advertising trackers.

How we use it

  • To run TwoCents: create your account, deliver your memos to the audience you choose, and show you memos from others.
  • To pick public memos for your Discover tab, based on who you follow and what you like and listen to.
  • To keep TwoCents safe: review reports, enforce our community rules, and prevent abuse.
  • To contact you about your account, for example password reset emails.

We don’t sell your personal information, and we don’t use it for advertising.

Who can see what

  • Your profile (name, @handle, follower and following counts and lists) is visible to people signed in to TwoCents.
  • Global memos can be heard by anyone signed in, and can be reposted to others’ followers.
  • Followers-only memos can be heard only by followers you’ve approved.
  • Close friends memos can be heard only by people on your close friends list. The list itself is private: only you can see it, and nobody is told when you add or remove them.
  • Comments, likes and reposts are visible to people who can hear the memo.
  • Your blocked list is private to you. Blocked people aren’t told.

If you remove a follower or take someone off your close friends list, they lose access to those memos right away, including older ones.

Service providers

TwoCents is built on Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage and Hosting), which stores and serves your account and content on our behalf under Google’s security and privacy terms. If you install TwoCents from the Apple App Store or Google Play, those stores also process information under their own policies.

Keeping and deleting your information

  • You can delete any of your memos or comments at any time.
  • You can delete your account in the app under Profile → Delete account. This removes your profile, memos, recordings, comments, reposts, follows, follow requests and lists from our active systems. Copies may remain for a short time in system logs or backups before being overwritten.
  • We may keep reports and related records for as long as needed to keep TwoCents safe or meet legal obligations.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, download or delete your personal information, or to object to certain uses. You can do most of this in the app; for anything else, email owner@snacktimemedia.net and we’ll respond within 30 days.

Children

TwoCents isn’t for children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, email us and we’ll delete it.

Security

Information is encrypted in transit, and access rules on our servers make sure each memo, recording and list can only be reached by the people allowed to see it. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

Changes to this policy

If we make meaningful changes, we’ll update the date above and let you know in the app before they take effect.
