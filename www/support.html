Common questions

How do I post a voice memo?

Tap Post, tap the red button to record (up to 5 minutes) and tap it again to stop. Play it back if you like, choose who can hear it (Close friends, Followers only or Global), add a caption and tap Post.

Recording says the microphone is blocked

On iPhone, open Settings → TwoCents (or Safari) → Microphone and turn it on. On Android, open Settings → Apps → TwoCents (or Chrome) → Permissions → Microphone. Then tap record again.

A memo won’t play

Check your connection and tap play again. If you see “You don’t have access to this memo anymore,” the person may have removed you as a follower or from their close friends list. If it keeps happening, email us with the message you see and what phone you use.

How do follow requests work?

Tapping Follow sends a request. Once the person approves it, you can hear their followers-only memos. You’ll see requests to you under Profile → Follow requests (a red dot on the Profile tab means someone’s waiting).

Who can see my close friends list?

Only you. Nobody is told when you add or remove them. Close friends memos go only to people on the list.

How do I remove a follower or block someone?

Open your Followers list from your profile and tap Remove, or open their profile and tap •••. Blocking also stops them from following you, commenting on your memos or sending you memos.

I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen, tap “Forgot password?” and enter your email. We’ll send a reset link (check your spam folder).

How do I delete my account?

Go to Profile → Delete account and enter your password. This permanently removes your profile, memos, recordings, comments, reposts, follows and lists.
