## Promotional text (170 max, can change anytime without review)

Say it out loud. Share quick voice memos with your close friends, your followers, or the whole world, and hear what your people are up to.

## Description

TwoCents is the easiest way to share what's on your mind, out loud.

Record a voice memo, pick who hears it, and post. Scroll through memos from your close friends and the people you follow, newest first, and play them back at the speed you like.

YOU DECIDE WHO HEARS IT
• Close friends: a private list only you can see. Nobody is told when you add or remove them.
• Followers only: for people you've approved to follow you.
• Global: public memos anyone can discover.

MADE FOR LISTENING
• Memos up to 5 minutes, with a live waveform while you record
• Press and hold play to switch between 1×, 1.25×, 1.5× and 2×
• Memos play one after another as you scroll

FIND YOUR PEOPLE
• Follow requests: you approve who follows you
• Discover public memos picked for you
• Like, comment, and repost public memos to your followers

SAFE BY DESIGN
• Report memos, comments or people, and block anyone, in two taps
• Reports are reviewed within 24 hours
• Delete your memos or your whole account anytime

Your two cents, in your own voice.
